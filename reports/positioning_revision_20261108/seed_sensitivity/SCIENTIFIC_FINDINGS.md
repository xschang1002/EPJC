# Post-hoc seed sensitivity: scientific interpretation

This analysis was added after the C/D outcomes were known. It describes the five existing paired optimizer seeds, conditional on the same source training data, fixed training fractions and checkpoint-selection rule. C and D evaluate the same trained models; they are not pooled into ten seeds. No training or inference was performed.

The main rise and fall are unanimous across the observed seeds. For both catalogs and both fractions, Coarse2-minus-Hidden5 is positive in all five seeds, and Coarse8-minus-Coarse2 is negative in all five. These observations support the broad non-monotonic profile across the observed optimizations. They do not establish a population law over new training datasets or a unique mechanism.

The final step has weaker optimizer agreement at the smaller training fraction. Full5-minus-Coarse8 at 10% has four negative seeds and one positive seed on both C and D. Its five-seed mean is −0.001237058 nat/event on C and −0.001444682 on D, with sample SD 0.002136545 and 0.002087145. At 100%, this step is negative in all five seeds on both catalogs. The Full5-minus-Hidden5 endpoint is also negative in all five seeds for both fractions and catalogs.

For the 10% final step, deleting each seed in turn yields mean ranges [−0.001957663, −0.000546547] on C and [−0.002119705, −0.000723095] on D. Thus no single observed seed determines its negative average, even though the individual seeds are not unanimous. More generally, all 32 catalog/contrast combinations retain their original five-seed mean direction in all five leave-one-seed-out summaries. These extrema are descriptive omission ranges, not confidence intervals or evidence that an unobserved optimizer run must have the same sign.

The direct Full5 gain at 10% likewise has four positive seeds and one negative seed on both catalogs. Its mean remains positive under every omission: [0.001331495, 0.002460921] on C and [0.001326596, 0.002467387] on D. All other direct gains have five positive seeds. The full tables preserve every sign and every original seed value.

The default paper display should be the mean, sample SD, individual-seed range/signs and leave-one-out range. The optional Student-t table is useful as an explicitly assumption-dependent check, but should not replace that display or the registered fixed-model event intervals. It assumes five independent paired optimizer-seed draws and an approximately normal distribution of seed-level contrasts, conditional on the fixed inputs. Five values cannot reliably establish that approximation. The intervals exclude training-dataset variation, simulator uncertainty and total training uncertainty, and are nominal rather than multiplicity-adjusted.

Under those optional assumptions, the t intervals include zero for the direct Full5 gain at 10% and Full5-minus-Coarse8 at 10%, on both catalogs. The C/D final-step intervals are [−0.003889929, 0.001415813] and [−0.004036214, 0.001146850]. This is compatible with their four-versus-one sign pattern and illustrates why narrow fixed-model event intervals must not be interpreted as confidence over optimizer variation. The other 28 nominal intervals exclude zero, without a simultaneous-family claim.

The separate catalog tables contain all 16 contrasts, not only the highlighted comparisons. `results/seed_sensitivity.csv` has 32 summaries, `paired_seed_values.csv` retains 160 seed values, and `leave_one_seed_out.csv` retains all 160 omission means. Effects use nat per eligible event; TeX tables multiply them by 1000 solely for readability.
